# RouteRadar
An end-to-end data engineering and analytics project that uses Transport for London data to explore London Undergroumd service disruptions and reliability.

---

## What I built and who is it for

RouteRadar is being developed as a commuter focused application that helps London Underground users explore historical service disruptions and understand the reliability of their favorite lines.

---

## Project status - Work in Progress

RouteRadar is currently in the early stages of development.

I have obtained Transport for Londons trains `trains-service-status-messages.csv` dataset, which will form the foundation of the inital data pipeline. It is worth noting that there is additional historical service-status datasets covering 2020-2025.

As development progresses, I will assess whether these additional datasets provide useful information beyond the main dataset and integrate them if appropriate. I will take into account overlapping records and data inconsistencies.

This repository represents an inital draft and will be updated.


---

## Datasets

Historial London Underground service-status data published by Transport for London.

Source: https://tfl.gov.uk/corporate/publications-and-reports/underground-services-performance
